"No Ads + Umbrella Pack" is a non-consumable In-App Purchase with two benefits.

1. No ads
The app shows only rewarded ads, and only when the player chooses to watch one:
- Easy, Normal and Hard: when the player runs out of apples, "Watch an Ad to Continue" lets them continue once.
- Challenge: the 1st, 4th, 7th, ... apple in each play is taken with "Watch an Ad to Take It".
After the purchase, these buttons become "Continue" and the apples are taken without an ad.

2. Umbrellas
Five umbrella designs for the piglets: Cherry Blossom, Sunflower, Maple Leaves, Snowflake and Polka Dot. The Leaf umbrella is free.

HOW TO PURCHASE
No login or account is required.
1. On the title screen, tap the umbrella button at the top right ("Umbrellas").
2. On the "Umbrellas" screen, tap "Buy for <price>" and confirm the purchase.
3. After the purchase, tap an umbrella to use it. The piglets carry it in the game.

HOW TO RESTORE
Tap "Restore Purchases" on the "Umbrellas" screen, or open Settings (the gear button on the title screen) and tap "Restore Purchases".
